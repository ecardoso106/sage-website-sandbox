# Get Help

Stuck? Here's where to go.

## 1. Check the FAQ

Many common problems, like SSH errors and dropped connections, are answered in the [FAQ](./faq).

## 2. Ask on Slack

Join the **Sage Community Slack** to ask questions and talk with other Sage users. To get an invitation, email [support@sagecontinuum.org](mailto:support@sagecontinuum.org).

## 3. Email support

For account problems, access requests, or anything else, email [support@sagecontinuum.org](mailto:support@sagecontinuum.org).

For more ways to reach the team, see [Contact us](../contact-us).
